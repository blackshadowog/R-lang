# Sales Forecasting — Business Report

## Executive Summary
Forecast monthly sales using historical trend and seasonality.

## Business Question
What sales level can the business expect over the next 12 months?

## Method
1. Prepare monthly data.
2. Hold out the latest 12 months.
3. Train Holt-Winters.
4. Evaluate MAE/RMSE.
5. Retrain on all data and forecast 12 months.

## Recommendations
Use forecasts for inventory, staffing, revenue planning and campaign timing. Monitor forecast error and add promotions/holidays for a production model.
